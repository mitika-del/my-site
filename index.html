How It Works

The Positive Pressure Principle

Fresh outside air. Precision-filtered through 4 stages. Pushed into every room. Stale air out.

01

Outside air enters

Fresh outdoor air is drawn in through a 200mm intake — never recirculated indoor air. Every breath your family takes is filtered outdoor air.

02

4-stage filtration

Pre-filter → Medium filter → HEPA H14 → Superior allergen filter. 99.997% particle capture down to 0.3 µm — the size that reaches your lungs.

03

Clean air fills every room

Purified air is pushed in under gentle positive pressure. It travels through corridors and door gaps — every room, simultaneously. No ducting. No false ceilings.

04

Polluted air is expelled

As clean air builds, stale and polluted air is pushed out through micro-gaps and exhaust vents. Your home breathes.
